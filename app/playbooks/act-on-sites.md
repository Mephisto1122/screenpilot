name: Carry out actions on sites and in apps
when: The task is to do something, not only to read: fill a form, book, order, upload, change a setting, send or publish.
words: fill|form|book|order|buy|purchase|upload|submit|register|sign up|apply|send|publish|post|schedule|cancel|change|update|set up
order: 9
---
- First learn the whole path: how many steps, what is asked, what cannot be undone. Read the page; do not guess from a button.
- Fill a form from top to bottom; after each field check that it holds what you typed (autocomplete and formats change things). Dates, phone numbers and amounts are where forms go wrong.
- A field that offers choices: open it and choose the entry; do not type over it.
- After every step look for the page's answer: a confirmation, a red message, a changed total. No visible answer means it did not happen; do not repeat blindly, check the state first (the list, the cart, the sent folder).
- Never submit twice. If unsure whether it went through, look for the result before trying again.
- Uploads and downloads: check the file name and that it finished.
- Stop before anything that sends, publishes, pays, deletes, accepts terms or changes an account, unless the task asked for exactly that; then say what will happen and what it costs.
- Use only the user's real details; a missing detail is a question for the user, not something to make up.
- At the end, prove it: the confirmation text or number, and where it can be seen again.
