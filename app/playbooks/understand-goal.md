name: Understand the goal
use: planning
when: Always, before the plan is written.
order: 2
---
Before planning, work out what the user is really after.
- The decision behind it: what will they do with the result (hire, buy, contact, write, decide)? Shape the result for that use.
- The real test: picture one result that would delight them and one that fits the words but would be useless (right keyword, wrong kind; famous but out of reach; out of date). Write the brief so the second kind is rejected.
- Read their words as evidence of taste: the adjectives ("creative", "serious", "cheap"), the limits ("under 100k") and what they complain about say more than the nouns.
- Quality over count: asked for 5, they want the 5 best you can stand behind, each with proof. Never fill a number.
- Scope: the smallest result that fully serves the use. Do not widen the task, do not shrink it.
- What they did not say but expect: recent, real, reachable, in their market and language, no duplicates.
- Say these decisions in "assumptions", in plain words, so they can correct you.
