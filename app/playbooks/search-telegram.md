name: Search in Telegram
when: The task is about Telegram channels, groups or posts: finding them, reading them or following a topic there.
words: telegram|t\.me|tgstat|channel
order: 5
---
- Without an account: a public channel's posts are at t.me/s/<name> in the browser (newest at the bottom, scroll up for older; the search field there searches inside that channel). One post is t.me/<name>/<number>.
- Finding channels: web search with site:t.me plus the subject, in the language the channel would use. Catalogues such as tgstat.com list channels by topic and country with subscriber numbers.
- In web.telegram.org (it needs the user's login: that is their turn, never yours): the search box finds public channels, groups and bots by name; the Posts tab searches public posts and may need Premium; inside a chat, search finds words and can be narrowed by date and by sender.
- Judge a channel before trusting it: subscribers, how long it has been posting, whether the organisation's own website links to it, a verified mark. Copies with similar names are common.
- The pinned post and the description usually hold the rules, the links and the linked discussion group.
- For each finding note: channel, post address, date, and the words that matter.
- Do not join, post, react, message anyone or start a bot unless the task asks for it and it was approved. Do not open files or download links from channels.
