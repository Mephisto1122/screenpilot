name: Find people, accounts and companies
when: The task is to find or shortlist professionals, creators, accounts, suppliers or companies, on a platform or on the web.
words: photographer|designer|creator|influencer|account|profile|supplier|agency|freelanc|candidate|shortlist|scout|company|companies|instagram|linkedin|tiktok
order: 4
---
- Search for the thing itself, where it presents itself: professionals where they show their work, companies on their own site and in registries; not on pages that only mention them.
- Three kinds of lead, by yield: (1) the platform's search with two or three plain words; (2) the neighbourhood of a good find: similar or suggested accounts, who it tags, credits, follows or is listed with; (3) lists others made: directories, award and festival pages, agency rosters, "top" articles, tag pages.
- Collect every candidate in view before opening any. Keep TO VISIT, VISITED (one word why not) and KEPT in the notebook; never open one twice.
- Judge in two passes: first the header (what they call themselves, size, place) and reject at once on a clear miss; only for the rest open the work itself.
- Proof comes from their own page: what they show, who they name, what is dated. A claim counts when a second place agrees (the client's page, a credit, a publication).
- Same name, different person: confirm with two matching details (place, field, photo, a link between the profiles).
- One good find is the best source of the next five. When finds dry up, go back to the best one and walk its neighbourhood.
- This is for professional and public presence. Do not look for a private person's home, family, phone or whereabouts; if the task needs that, stop and say so.
