      Tasks run until they are done or you press Stop. Set a limit below only if you want one; leave it empty for no limit.

      Automatic uses Claude when it has a key, otherwise GPT. Used by "Smart & private" (it gets a text description of the screen; screenshots stay on this Mac) and by "Fastest" (it sees the screenshots).

      Luna is fine for simple, repetitive work. For tasks that need judgment (which results are good, when to stop looking) Terra is the one built for working a computer from screenshots; a long task costs about ten times what it does on Luna.

      With Terra, Sol or Astra chosen above: Luna does the clicking, typing and moving around, at a tenth of the price or less. The model you chose still makes the plan, checks every step, takes over when Luna gets stuck, and takes every step that records a finding or writes the result, so what is kept and what is rejected stays its judgment.

      Off: it works alone. When something does not work it tries another way, then makes a new plan, then leaves that part and goes on, and it always reports what it found and what it could not do.
